1 · Import Permit / Import Approval
When: Complete Scooter Import
MITI AP Micromobility
Import permit to bring complete electric scooters into Malaysia — HS code, MITI pre-check, manual approval, ePermit and quota.
View guide → 📄 AP flowchart 📄 Doc checklist
When: Battery Imported Separately
MITI Battery AP
Import permit for scooter lithium batteries brought in separately under HS 87.01–87.11 (effective 1 Jan 2026). Now applied via ePermit and needs a Customs Classification Letter (Customs Ruling) + Technical Evaluation Report (UN 38.3 + UN R100/R136 + IEC); processed in 5 working days.
View guide → 📄 MITI Battery SOP
2 · Product Safety / Certification
When: Charger / Adapter
ST CoA + ST-SIRIM Label
Electrical-safety approval for the scooter charger/adaptor (ST Category 33) — ST CoA, SIRIM consignment test, then ST-SIRIM label before sale.
View guide → 📄 Electrical equipment guidelines
When: Wireless Function
SIRIM / MCMC eComM Approval
Communications approval when the scooter has any wireless module (Bluetooth, WiFi, GPS, 4G/SIM, app control) — choose Type, Hybrid or Special Approval.
View guide → 📄 Type Approval 📄 Special Approval
When: Secondary Rechargeable Battery
KPDN MC Mark / Enquiry
Mandatory safety-mark check / enquiry for rechargeable lithium batteries sold in or with scooters; highest risk when sold as a separate spare.
View guide → 📄 Garis Panduan 2018
When: Battery Safety & Documents
MS IEC 62133 / IEC 62133-2 · UN 38.3 · SDS/MSDS
All three lithium-battery documents in one guide — battery safety (MS IEC 62133 / IEC 62133-2), transport/shipping safety (UN 38.3) and dangerous-goods handling (SDS/MSDS) — and how to match them to the shipment.
View guide → 📄 SIRIM battery req. 📄 Garis Panduan 2018
3 · Shipping / Transport Documents
Lithium-battery shipping (UN 38.3) and safety handling (SDS/MSDS) are documented inside the Battery Safety & Documents guide in section 2 above — they are part of the same combined battery document set.
4 · Product Claim
When: IP54 / IP65 Claimed
IP Test Report (IEC 60529 / MS IEC 60529)
Test report needed to back any dust/water (IP) rating claimed on the listing, label or packaging, and how to avoid over-claiming.
